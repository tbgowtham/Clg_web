About Sridevi Arts & Science College

A Legacy of Purposeful Rural & Urban Education.

Sridevi Arts and Science College is a leading co-educational institution in Ponneri, established in the year 2001 with a visionary commitment to impart purposeful and accessible higher education to aspiring students, particularly those hailing from rural backgrounds.

Affiliated to the prestigious University of Madras, the college has grown into a distinguished center of learning offering 8 Undergraduate and 2 Postgraduate departments, alongside robust Physical Education and Career Placement cells. With dedicated faculty, high-tech laboratories, and strong merit scholarship support, we empower over 1,500 students each year.
